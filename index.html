// WHY KAUSTHUBA

1 IDEA. 1 STORY. 1 FILM.

Beyond Just Making an Advertisement.

Brands can promote their products in many ways, from live-action shoots and traditional animation to influencer marketing and AI-generated visuals. Kausthuba takes a different approach: we understand the brand, build a story around its value, and use AI-assisted animation to bring that story to life.

01 / LIVE-ACTION COMPARISON Reason One

We Are Not a Physical Production Setup.

A traditional live-action shoot involves actors, locations, cameras, lighting, equipment, and a heavy production crew. Instead, we build the entire story around animated characters and designed 3D environments, eliminating physical production friction when the concept suits animation.

PHYSICAL PRODUCTION → ANIMATION-LED PRODUCTION 01 OF 05
02 / MANUAL ANIMATION COMPARISON Reason Two

We Are Not a Manual 3D Animation House.

Traditional animation requires extensive, months-long artist modeling, manual keyframing, rendering, and heavy overhead. Instead, we leverage generative AI tools under strict creative direction to generate and refine visual scenes rapidly without sacrificing quality.

MANUAL ANIMATION WORKFLOW → AI-ASSISTED WORKFLOW 02 OF 05
03 / INFLUENCER PROMOTION COMPARISON Reason Three

We Are Not an Influencer Sponsorship Network.

Influencer campaigns introduce products through an individual's personal recommendations and audience reach. Instead, we make your brand the main hero of the story, engineering an animated narrative that directly addresses customer problems and proves your value.

CREATOR-LED PROMOTION → BRAND-LED STORYTELLING 03 OF 05
04 / GENERIC AI COMPARISON Reason Four

We Are Not a Random Prompt Generator.

Generating clips without a creative strategy leaves a brand with flashy visual noise that fails to communicate value. Instead, we start with story architecture first, diagnosing your business differentiator before generating a single frame or scene.

PROMPT-LED GENERATION → STORY-LED CREATION 04 OF 05
05 / FINISHED FILM MASTER Reason Five

We Are Creators of One Connected Brand Film.

Instead of isolated video pieces or generic template clips designed around a specific promotional format, we bring every planned scene together with software timeline editing, music, and sound effects to deliver a complete story-led advertisement in 4K 60FPS.

INDIVIDUAL CONTENT PIECES → COMPLETE STORY-LED FILM 05 OF 05

WHAT YOU NEED
TO KNOW

What is Kausthuba, and what do you create?

Kausthuba is an AI-assisted creative storytelling studio for brand advertising. We turn a brand’s customer problem, solution, and unique value into an engaging visual story, then bring it to life through animated scenes, editing, music, and sound.

How does the Kausthuba process work?

We start by understanding your brand, audience, and what makes your solution different. Then we develop the story, plan the scenes, generate and refine the visuals, and assemble everything into a finished advertisement with music and sound effects.

How long does it take to create an advertisement?

Most projects follow a 7-day production plan, from brand discovery to final quality checks. More complex stories may take up to 11 days, depending on the creative requirements, additional refinement, and revisions.

How much does a Kausthuba advertisement cost?

Pricing depends on the story, video duration, and production complexity. Once we understand your brand and requirements, we can suggest a suitable creative direction and provide a project-specific quote.

If you have questions or need more details, feel free to reach out.
